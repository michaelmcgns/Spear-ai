# Spear Cold Caller Recruiting Gameplan

## The Model

You recruit commission-only cold callers to work your 25k lead list.
Their job: call agents/managers, qualify them, book a demo on your calendar.
Your job: run the demo, close the deal.

**Your cost: $0 unless they book a demo that shows.**

---

## Compensation Structure

| Action | Pay |
|--------|-----|
| Demo booked + prospect shows | **$25** |
| Demo that converts to paying customer | **$50 bonus** (on top of the $25) |
| Monthly bonus — 20+ shows in a month | **+$200** |

Keep it simple. Pay weekly via Venmo/Zelle. No shows, no pay — motivates them to qualify properly.

---

## Where to Find Callers

**Best sources (in order):**

1. **Your own network** — Other agents who want side income. They already know the product and can speak credibly to prospects.
2. **Facebook Groups** — "Sales Jobs Remote", "Commission Sales Reps Wanted", "Work From Home Sales"
3. **Indeed** — Post a "Remote Commission Sales Rep" listing, $25/qualified demo, no base
4. **Craigslist** — "Gigs > Sales" section in major cities. Free to post.
5. **Upwork** — Filter for cold callers with sales experience, pay per demo booked

**What to say in your posting:**
> "Looking for experienced phone sales reps to book demos for an AI software product targeting life insurance agents. Commission only — $25 per attended demo, $75 if they buy. Work your own hours, provided script and leads. Must have sales experience and reliable phone."

---

## Onboarding (Keep It Simple)

Send each new caller:
1. The **one-pager PDF** (so they understand the product)
2. The **call script** below
3. A shared **Google Sheet** to log calls + demo bookings
4. Your **Calendly link** to book demos directly

Do a 20-minute Zoom with each new caller to walk through the script. That's it.

---

## The Call Script (Give This to Your Callers)

---

**OPENING**

*"Hey, is this [Name]?"*
*(wait)*
*"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] — I'm reaching out because I work with a company called Spear. We built an AI tool specifically for life insurance agents that coaches them in real time during calls — I'm talking NEPQ questions, objection responses, buyer psychology — all showing up on their screen while they're on the phone. You familiar with anything like that?"*
*(wait for response)*

---

**IF INTERESTED / CURIOUS:**

*"Yeah so basically it listens to the call, identifies what kind of buyer you're talking to, and tells you exactly what to say before the silence gets awkward. Agents using it are seeing close rates go up 30-40% in the first month. We're doing a quick 20-minute demo this week — I've got a spot on [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] — which works better for you?"*

---

**IF "WHAT DOES IT COST?":**

*"It's $49 a month per agent — honestly most agents make that back on one extra close. The demo is free, no commitment, takes 20 minutes. Worth a look — which day works better for you?"*

---

**IF "I'M NOT INTERESTED":**

*"Totally fair — quick question before I let you go, do you manage a team at all or know any agency owners who might be open to it? I'll send you $25 just for a warm intro if they show up."*

---

**IF "SEND ME INFO":**

*"I'll send it over — what's the best email? And while I have you, I can pencil you in tentatively for [day/time] and if the info looks good you just keep the appointment, if not no hard feelings. Which day works?"*

---

**OBJECTION: "I already have a system"**

*"What are you using right now?"*
*(listen)*
*"Got it — does it coach you during the call or after? Because that's the difference. Most tools analyze after the fact. Spear is literally talking to you in real time. 20 minutes — worth a look?"*

## Tracking Sheet (Google Sheet Columns)

| Lead Name | Phone | Date Called | Outcome | Demo Booked | Demo Date | Showed? | Sold? | Caller Name |
|-----------|-------|-------------|---------|-------------|-----------|---------|-------|-------------|

Share this with all callers. Review it weekly. Fire callers with less than 5 bookings in 2 weeks.

---

## Managing Your Team

- **Weekly check-in**: 15-min group Zoom every Monday. Share what's working.
- **Leaderboard**: Post the top 3 callers each week in a group chat. Friendly competition works.
- **Quality control**: Listen to 2-3 calls per caller per week (use a call recorder app). Correct bad habits early.
- **Cut fast**: If someone isn't booking after 2 weeks, replace them. Don't carry dead weight.

## The Math

| Callers | Calls/Day Each | Total Calls/Day | Demo Rate | Shows/Day | Monthly Shows | Close Rate | New Customers/Mo | MRR Added |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|-----------|-----------|---------------|------------|------------------|-----------|
| 5 | 80 | 400 | 3% | 12 | 240 | 25% | 60 | $2,940 |
| 10 | 80 | 800 | 3% | 24 | 480 | 25% | 120 | $5,880 |
| 20 | 80 | 1,600 | 3% | 48 | 960 | 25% | 240 | $11,760 |

**Your cost at 10 callers, 480 shows/mo:** $25 × 480 = $12,000 + bonuses
**Revenue at 25% close:** 120 customers × $49 = $5,880 MRR first month, compounding every month after.

By month 3 with 10 callers you're cash-flow positive on a growing MRR base.

---

## First 7 Days Action Plan

- **Day 1**: Post on 3 Facebook groups + Indeed. Text 5 people in your network.
- **Day 2-3**: Interview/onboard first 3-5 callers. Send them the script + one-pager.
- **Day 4**: Everyone starts calling. You run your first demos.
- **Day 5-7**: Adjust script based on what's working. Replace anyone not booking.
- **End of week**: You should have 10-20 demos booked and your first paying customers.
