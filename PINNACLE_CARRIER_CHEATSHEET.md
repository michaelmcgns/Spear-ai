# 📋 PINNACLE — Carrier Cheat Sheet
*What each carrier is best for. Built for mortgage-protection telesales. Confirm exact products + current underwriting niches with Chase / the carrier guides — niches and products change.*

> **How to use on a call:** client gives you age + health + budget + goal (pay off mortgage vs. leave money vs. cash value) → you instantly know which 2 carriers to quote. Speed + certainty = trust = close.

---

## ⚡ QUICK PICK (by situation)
- **Standard mortgage protection term, healthy-ish, fast issue** → **Americo**, **Mutual of Omaha**, **American Amicable**
- **Lowest possible term price (healthy shopper comparing)** → **LGA / Banner (Legal & General)**, **SBLI**
- **Term + living benefits / return of premium (ROP)** → **American Amicable**, **Americo**, **Lifeshield**
- **Cash value / accumulation upsell (IUL)** → **Mutual of Omaha (IUL Express)**, **North American**, **F&G**
- **Final expense / older / health-challenged (whole life)** → **Liberty Bankers**, **Foresters**, **Transamerica**, **Royal Neighbors**, **American Home Life (Aetna)**, **Trinity Life**
- **Guaranteed issue / decline-proof backup** → **Corebridge (GIWL)**, **GTL**, **Mutual of Omaha (GI)**
- **Instant-decision marketplace term** → **Quility / LGA (Quility)**, **SBLI**

---

## 🏆 YOUR CORE MORTGAGE-PROTECTION CARRIERS (lead with these)
**Mutual of Omaha (MOO)** — the gold standard. Term Life Express + IUL Express, no-exam simplified issue, fast/instant decisions, built-in living benefits, A+ brand name that *helps you close*. Great for clean-to-moderate health. Your go-to for term AND your IUL upsell.

**Americo** — bread-and-butter mortgage protection term. Simplified-issue, instant-decision telesales, ROP (return-of-premium) options clients love, lenient on standard health. Fast and reliable — perfect for the typical homeowner.

**American Amicable (Am-Am)** — strong term with **living benefits + ROP** (Home Certainty / Family Choice family). Good for tobacco users and moderate health. Instant decision, telesales-friendly. Excellent value-stack to present.

**Lifeshield** — term + final expense with living benefits, built for mortgage-protection telesales. Solid alternative when MOO/Americo aren't the fit.

---

## 💰 LOWEST-COST TERM (for the price shopper / healthy client)
**LGA — Legal & General America / Banner (incl. "LGA (Quility)")** — routinely the **cheapest term rates** in the industry for healthy clients. When someone's healthy and comparing prices, this wins. OPTerm products, strong accelerated underwriting.

**SBLI** — competitive term + whole life with **accelerated (no-exam) underwriting** for healthy applicants up to certain ages/face amounts. Fast and well-priced.

**Quility / LGA (Quility)** — Quility is the marketplace/platform: **instant-decision level term**. Use for a quick, clean issue when you want speed.

---

## 📈 CASH VALUE / IUL (the upsell when they want money back, not just term)
**Mutual of Omaha — IUL Express** — simplified-issue IUL, no exam, living benefits. Easiest IUL to place in telesales. Your primary accumulation play.

**North American (NACOLAH)** — strong **IUL for accumulation** (Builder Plus) + competitive term. For clients focused on building cash value.

**F&G (Fidelity & Guaranty)** — IUL + annuities, strong on **accumulation/retirement** money. Good for the client thinking long-term wealth, not just protection.

**Mutual Trust Life (MTL)** — participating whole life (dividends) + IUL. For the client who wants permanent, guaranteed, dividend-paying coverage.

**Kansas City Life** — broad portfolio (term/UL/whole). A utility carrier — useful for odd cases that don't fit the specialists.

**Corebridge Financial (formerly AIG)** — Select-a-Term + **Guaranteed Issue Whole Life**. Good term option and a reliable GI fallback.

---

## ⚰️ FINAL EXPENSE / SIMPLIFIED WHOLE LIFE (older or health-challenged)
When a client is older (often 50–85) or has health issues that knock out term, pivot to small whole life:

**Liberty Bankers (LBL)** — strong, competitively-priced **final expense whole life**, good for older / health-challenged, day-1 coverage options. (Quote tool: https://www.tappsolutions.com/calculators/calc-lbl-ord/web/index.html)

**Foresters** — fraternal; **PlanRight** simplified-issue whole life + term, accepts some impaired risk, member benefits (great closing extra). Solid FE + moderate-health term.

**Transamerica** — final expense whole life + term; known to be **lenient on certain builds/health conditions**. Good when health is borderline.

**Royal Neighbors** — fraternal; simplified-issue whole life + term, **lenient on some impaired risk**, member benefits. Good FE option, historically strong for women.

**American Home Life (Aetna)** — competitive **final expense whole life** (Patriot series). Another FE option to rate-shop.

**Trinity Life** — simplified-issue **final expense whole life**. FE niche / rate comparison.

**GTL (Guarantee Trust Life)** — simplified & guaranteed issue, supplemental/accident products. Use for **hard-to-place** or supplemental needs.

## 🎯 RULES OF THUMB
1. **Quote 2 carriers, recommend 1.** Options build trust; one clear recommendation gets the close.
2. **Match product to their words:** "pay off the house" = term; "leave something / money back" = ROP or whole life; "build money" = IUL.
3. **Health first, then carrier.** Knowing the niche map above means you place them on the first call instead of "let me get back to you."
4. **Brand names close.** With nervous buyers, lead with Mutual of Omaha — the name does work for you.
5. **Always confirm** current product + underwriting with Chase / the carrier's field guide before you quote — this sheet is your map, not gospel.
