## 7) THE CALL FRAMEWORK (NEPQ for mortgage protection)
This is the spine. Layer Chase's exact wording on top, but never skip a stage.

**Stage 1 — Connect (lower defenses).** Calm, curious, slightly detached. You're the doctor, not the salesman.
> *"Hey [name], real quick — you reached out about protecting your mortgage. I just had a couple questions to see if I can even help, is that fair?"*

**Stage 2 — Situation (gather facts = the fact-finder, section 7a).** Ask, listen, write. Let them talk.

**Stage 3 — Problem awareness (let them feel the gap).**
> *"So if something happened to you tomorrow — God forbid — who's making that mortgage payment? What happens to [spouse/kids] and the house?"*
> *"Have you got anything in place for that right now, or is this the thing that's been on the back burner?"*

**Stage 4 — Solution awareness (paint the fix in their words).**
> *"So what you're really wanting is — if you're not here, the house is paid off and your family stays put. Did I get that right?"*

**Stage 5 — Present (prescribe — section 7b).** Now and only now, quote. Tie every number to what THEY said.

**Stage 6 — Commit (assume the close — section 7c).**

### 7a) FACT-FINDER (write every answer in your notebook)
- Full name · date of birth / age · state
- **Mortgage:** balance · monthly payment · years left · lender
- **Who** are we protecting? (spouse, kids, co-signer) — get names, use them
- **Health:** height/weight · tobacco? · conditions (diabetes, BP, heart, cancer hx) · meds
- **Coverage in place?** through work? amount?
- **Budget:** *"What were you hoping to keep this around per month?"*

### 7b) PRESENTATION (prescribe, don't dump)
- Recommend **one** primary option (the right carrier from your cheat sheet) + maybe a step-down.
- Frame in their language: *"This pays off the $[balance] mortgage so [spouse] never has to worry — for about $[X]/month, less than [a tank of gas / streaming bundle]."*
- **Anchor high, then fit the budget:** present the full-coverage number first, then right-size to their comfort. Never lead with the cheapest.
- Use **living benefits** if the carrier has them (covers critical/chronic illness too) — huge value-add.

### 7c) THE CLOSE (assume it)
> *"Good news — based on what you told me, you qualify. Let's get you protected. I just need to confirm a few details to lock in the rate."*
- Go straight into the **e-application**. Don't pause for permission.
- Collect: beneficiary, bank/payment info, ID/SSN, health questions.
- **Trial close earlier:** *"If we can get this to fit your budget, is there any reason you wouldn't want to get your family protected today?"*

## 9) OBJECTION HANDLING (memorize these — log new ones)
Posture: agree → ask → reframe. Never argue. Stay calm and curious.

**"I need to talk to my spouse."**
> *"100% — this is a decision you should make together. Are they around now? Let's get them on for 2 minutes so you both have the facts and aren't relying on you to explain insurance later."*

**"It's too expensive."**
> *"I hear you. Compared to what though — the mortgage your family's stuck with if you're gone? Let's find a number that protects them AND fits your budget. What's comfortable for you per month?"* (then right-size coverage)

**"I already have coverage."**
> *"Smart — most people I talk to do. A lot of times it's through work and disappears if you leave the job, or it doesn't cover the full mortgage. Mind if I check the gap real quick so you know you're actually covered?"*

**"Let me think about it."**
> *"Totally fair. Just so I understand — is it the coverage amount, the price, or something about how it works that you want to think on?"* (isolate the real objection, handle it)

**"Send me some info."**
> *"I can do better than a brochure you won't read — let me show you your actual numbers right now, takes 4 minutes. If it's not a fit, no harm."*

**"How'd you get my info?"**
> *"You filled out a request online about protecting your mortgage — totally fine if the timing's changed. While I've got you, did you ever get that handled?"*

> Log every real objection + the response that worked in your notebook → review weekly.
