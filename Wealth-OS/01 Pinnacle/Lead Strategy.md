# 🎯 Lead Strategy

## What you have
- **500 aged "60+ month" leads @ $0.40** — you already paid. Hard to close, BUT free reps and the occasional close. **Mine these daily right now.**
- **Fresh "3-month" leads @ $10** — much higher intent, much easier to close. **Buy these the moment you have commission to reinvest.**

## The sequence (because you have $0 today)
1. **Now:** Dial the 500 aged leads hard. Goal = reps + a few closes to generate first cash. Expect low contact/close — that's fine, it's free practice and free money.
2. **First commissions:** Don't spend them. Reinvest into **$10 fresh leads**.
3. **Scale:** As fresh leads close, ladder up the daily lead budget. Track ROI per source in [[Sales Tracker]].

## Rules
- **Speed to lead:** fresh lead in < 5 minutes beats one called an hour later, every time.
- **Aged-lead angle:** "Hey, you reached out a while back about protecting your mortgage — did you ever get that handled?" (assume nothing, re-open the loop).
- **Never let a lead die at one touch:** 5–7 touches across call + text + voicemail.

## Daily lead targets
- [ ] 100 aged dials/day (free)
- [ ] Every fresh lead called < 5 min
- [ ] 0 leads with only 1 touch
