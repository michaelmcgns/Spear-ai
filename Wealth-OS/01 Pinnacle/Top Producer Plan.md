# 🏆 Top Producer Plan

**The bar at Pinnacle:** ~**$100k AP/month** and **70+ policies/month**. That's the summit. You don't start there — you climb in stages.

## The math that matters
- Mortgage protection avg AP ≈ **$1,000–$1,200/policy**.
- **$30k personal AP** unlocks **90% comp** (from 70%). This is your FIRST mission — it makes every future sale ~29% bigger.
- $30k ÷ ~$1,100 ≈ **~27 policies** to unlock 90%.
- Top producer (~$100k/mo) ≈ **~3–4 closes/day, every day**.

## The ladder (laddered milestones)
- [ ] **M1 — First $1k AP week** (prove the system works)
- [ ] **M2 — 5 policies total** (rhythm)
- [ ] **M3 — $30k personal AP → 90% COMP UNLOCKED** 🔓 (top priority)
- [ ] **M4 — 10 policies/month** (consistency)
- [ ] **M5 — 30 policies/month** ($30k+/mo run-rate)
- [ ] **M6 — 70 policies / $100k AP month → TOP PRODUCER** 🏆

## The 7 levers to get there (do these, in order)
1. **Finish the 13 missing carrier contracts** — you can only sell what you're contracted for. More carriers = fit more clients = more closes. Free to do. → [[Carrier Contracts]]
2. **Own the script.** Drill Chase's script daily until tonality is automatic. Record yourself.
3. **Volume of dials.** New + broke = dial more than anyone. 100+ aged dials/day minimum.
4. **Speed to lead.** Any fresh lead → call in under 5 minutes.
5. **Follow-up machine.** 5–7 touches per lead (call + text + voicemail). Fortune is here.
6. **Reinvest.** Every commission → buy $10 fresh leads. Compound it.
7. **Review tape.** Log every call, review losses weekly, fix one thing. → [[Sales Tracker]]
