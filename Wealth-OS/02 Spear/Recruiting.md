## Where to find them (you're already inside the pond)
1. **Pinnacle agents & managers** — you talk to them daily. Every one is a Spear prospect. (Confirm with Chase what's allowed.)

3. Convert beta → **paid seat** once they feel the value.
4. Ask each happy user for **1 referral** (ideally a manager).
