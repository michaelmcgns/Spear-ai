## Funnel
1. **Seed (now):** 5–10 free beta agents from your network → testimonials + real data.
2. **Proof:** capture before/after (close rate, confidence) → 2–3 short case studies.
3. **Sell:** DM managers with the proof → book a 15-min demo → free trial → paid.
4. **Referral loop:** every happy user → 1 intro (target managers).
5. **Content (later, w/ the brand):** founder content once Italy brand is live.
