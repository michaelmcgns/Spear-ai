# Sales Setup — Get Organized Tonight 🎯

Goal: tomorrow you sit down and the only variable is your performance. Everything else is already handled. Sell like the expert.

---

## 1. Dialing station (the cockpit)
- [ ] **Dialer / phone system** logged in and tested (ask Chase what the team uses — power dialer? a softphone?). Make a test call to yourself.
- [ ] **Quality headset** — hands-free so you can talk with your hands + take notes. Test the mic.
- [ ] **Quiet room**, door closed, no background noise. Internet solid; phone hotspot as backup.
- [ ] **Two screens or split screen**: dialer/CRM on one side, script + objection sheet on the other.
- [ ] **Water + coffee** within reach. A mirror in view (you smile more, you sell more).

## 2. Leads — loaded and sorted (do this tonight)
- [ ] Aged list **imported into the dialer/CRM**, duplicates removed.
- [ ] **Sort by time zone / state** so you call people at a sane local hour (huge for contact rate).
- [ ] A clear **disposition system**: No Answer · Callback · Not Interested · Appointment · Sold. Use it on every call.
- [ ] Know your **call windows**: best contact = mid-morning and 4–7pm *their* local time.

## 3. Know your product cold (this is what makes you sound "high level")
- [ ] List your **6 active carriers** and one line on each: who they're best for (age, health, budget).
- [ ] **Underwriting cheat sheet**: which carrier accepts which health conditions (diabetes, BP, smoker, etc.). This is how you place a client on the spot instead of fumbling.
- [ ] Rough **rate ranges** for common ages/coverage so you can ballpark fast.
- [ ] **Quoting tool / carrier portals** open and logged in.
- [ ] Ask Chase: "Which of my 6 should I lead with, and what's the e-app process to submit a sale?"

## 4. Fact-finder (your on-call form)
Have this ready to fill on every contact — it controls the call and feeds the close:
- Name · age · state
- Mortgage balance + years left
- Beneficiary (who are we protecting?)
- Health: conditions, meds, height/weight, tobacco?
- Monthly budget they're comfortable with

## 5. Script + objections (within arm's reach)
- [ ] **Chase's script** printed or open. Read the open + close out loud 3× tonight.
- [ ] **Objection sheet** visible: "talk to my spouse," "too expensive," "already have coverage."
- [ ] Frame: you're the **doctor, not the salesman** — diagnose (fact-find), then prescribe. Calm, curious, certain.

## 6. The close mechanics (so a "yes" doesn't slip away)
- [ ] Know the **e-application** link/flow for your top carriers.
- [ ] Know what you collect to submit: **bank/payment info, beneficiary, ID/SSN**.
- [ ] Have your **calendar open** to book follow-ups/appointments instantly.

## 7. Tracking (one simple sheet)
- [ ] A running tally for the day: **Dials · Contacts · Appts · Closes · AP**.
- [ ] Jot every objection you hear → review tonight, sharpen tomorrow.

---

## Text Chase tonight (get answers before 8am)
> "Getting set to grind tomorrow. Quick Qs: 1) what dialer do we use? 2) which of my carriers should I lead with for mortgage protection? 3) what's the e-app process to submit a sale? 4) can I send 3 contracts in tomorrow — links?"

## The high-level mindset (free, decisive)
- **Volume + calm.** New guys rush and sound nervous. Slow your voice, lead the call.
- **Ask, don't pitch.** Fact-find first; people buy when they say it, not when you say it.
- **Assume the close.** "Let's get you protected — I just need a few details."
- **Every no is a rep.** You're sharpening for the $10 leads.

> Set it all up tonight. Tomorrow, just perform.
